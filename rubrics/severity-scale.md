# Severity Scale

## S1 - Small Inaccuracy

Slightly worsens the position, but the idea is understandable.

## S2 - Meaningful Inaccuracy

Misses a better plan, worsens piece placement, or loses some initiative.

## S3 - Serious Mistake

Changes the evaluation significantly or creates a lasting weakness.

## S4 - Blunder Or Major Strategic Failure

Loses material, allows a tactic, ruins a winning position, or enters a bad endgame.

## S5 - Repeated High-Impact Pattern

A recurring failure that explains multiple lost or spoiled games.

## Repeat Multiplier

First occurrence: 1.0x

Second similar occurrence: 1.5x

Third similar occurrence: promote to [GAP_LOG.md](/Users/arjun/dev/chess-improvement-pls/GAP_LOG.md)

## Bias

Prioritize repeated S3, S4, and S5 patterns.

Do not spend system energy cataloging every S1.
