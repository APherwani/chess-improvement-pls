# Critical Moment Rubric

A critical moment is a position where the game could change direction because of:

- tactical sharpness
- king safety change
- major trade decision
- pawn break
- transition into an endgame
- conversion chance
- defensive resource
- clock pressure
- opening move-order uncertainty
- opponent threat that needs concrete attention

## Before Engine

For each critical moment, record:

- move number
- FEN if available
- candidate moves considered
- move you would choose now without engine
- evaluation guess
- line calculated
- time spent
- what felt unclear

## After Engine

Compare:

- whether it was actually critical
- whether your evaluation was close
- whether your candidate list missed the key idea
- whether time spent matched position difficulty
