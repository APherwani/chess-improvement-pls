# Pawns Can Be The Biggest Piece

Source: G001.

## Model

In crowded middlegames, pieces visually dominate the board, but a single pawn move can be the most important threat.

## Trigger

- The opponent has an advanced pawn.
- I am about to develop, reroute, or activate a piece.
- A pawn move could create a passer, open a file, or fix a weakness.

## Question

What pawn move is coming down the pipeline, and does it matter more than my piece activity?

## G001 Evidence

After 14.dxc5, White's b-pawn was threatening b4-b5. I played 14...Nbd7 to activate a knight, but engine review showed 14...axb4 solved the pawn problem. Once b5 happened, the b-pawn became a major strategic issue.

## Repair

Before improving a piece in a crowded position, scan the opponent's pawns first:

- advanced pawns
- pawn breaks
- passed-pawn candidates
- pawn moves that attack my structure
