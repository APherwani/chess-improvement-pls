# No Automatic Move Chains

Source: G001.

## Model

One automatic move can be fine. Two or three automatic moves in a row can hide the moment where the position changes.

If the previous move was natural and quick, the next natural move must earn a short stop.

## Trigger

- The opponent is playing fast.
- I have plenty of time.
- I am about to make another normal-looking move.
- The move changes pawn structure, releases tension, or permits a pawn break.

## Question

What obvious move is coming down the pipeline for my opponent?

## G001 Evidence

Around 12...a5 and 13...bxc5, I made natural queenside moves without fully pricing White's b5-b6 plan. The engine did not say the whole sequence was losing, but it confirmed that 12...a5 was a meaningful structural concession and that White's queenside plan became the central issue.

## Repair

Before making a second automatic move in a row, pause and name:

- the opponent's most obvious reply
- the pawn break I am allowing
- the passed pawn or structural weakness that could result
