# GOALS.md

## Rating Goals

Current level: 1710 chess.com rapid.

First goal: 1850 chess.com rapid.

Stretch goal: 2000 chess.com rapid, only if progress and enjoyment remain strong.

## Improvement Goal

Reduce recurring self-inflicted losses:

- tactical oversights
- candidate-move failures
- misread opponent threats
- misevaluated exchanges
- poor conversion
- time-pressure decisions
- strategic autopilot
- opening traps caused by game-relevant knowledge gaps
- endgame failures that appear in real games

## What Counts As Progress

- Fewer repeated severe misses.
- Better recognition of critical moments.
- Better time usage in sharp positions.
- Improved conversion of better positions.
- Fewer opening traps.
- Clearer middlegame plans after the opening.
- More engine recommendations translated into human explanations.
- Rating trend over larger samples, not daily fluctuations.

## What Does Not Count As Progress

- Watching chess videos without applying them.
- Memorizing opening lines not connected to your games.
- Collecting engine lines you cannot explain.
- Doing random puzzles without connecting them to miss patterns.
- Playing extra games to escape review.
- Adding abstract notes that do not change future decisions.

## Operating Question

What kind of player am I proving myself to be across my games?
