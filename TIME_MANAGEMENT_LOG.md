# TIME_MANAGEMENT_LOG.md

Track whether time usage matches position difficulty.

## Patterns To Watch

- moving too quickly at the first sharp moment
- spending too long in familiar openings
- calculating only after the position gets worse
- panic decisions under 2 minutes
- blitzing recaptures
- overthinking safe positions
- avoiding calculation in unclear positions
- making 2 to 3 automatic moves in a row while there is still plenty of time
