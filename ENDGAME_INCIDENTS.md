# ENDGAME_INCIDENTS.md

Endgame study is triggered by your own games.

Do not create broad endgame theory notes unless a real game shows the theme matters.

## Trigger Rules

Log an endgame incident when:

- I failed to convert a clearly better endgame.
- I failed to hold a defensible endgame.
- I traded into a king-and-pawn ending without calculating the result.
- I allowed counterplay in a winning minor-piece endgame.
- I did not know whether to keep rooks or trade.
- I rushed pawn pushes instead of improving king position.

## Required Output

Each endgame incident should produce one of:

- one principle
- one model position
- one repair drill from the actual game
